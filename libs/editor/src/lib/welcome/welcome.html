A deck is a set of pages. Each page holds square keys that run actions on your computer: shortcuts, apps, websites, media keys and terminal commands.

  1. Add a page. Pick a preset such as Music or Coding, built from the apps on your computer, or start blank.
  2. Fill it. Add standard buttons from the catalog or make your own with an icon, a color and a list of actions.
  3. Use it. Swipe up and down between pages, left and right between sets. Pages can follow the app that has the focus.

The menu at the top left has the catalog, themes and settings.
